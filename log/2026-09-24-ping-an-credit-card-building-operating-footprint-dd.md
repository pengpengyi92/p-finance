# Ping An Credit Card Building — Public Operating Footprint DD

Date: 2026-09-24
Entity: Ping An Bank Credit Card Center
Location: Qianhai, Shenzhen
Evidence policy: public-source only

## Thesis

The Ping An Credit Card Building is best understood as a **named headquarters / anchor-tenant complex**, not as a building occupied exclusively by the Credit Card Center.

## Verified public facts

- Official Qianhai planning: 140,000 sqm planned GFA, including 127,220 sqm office and 12,780 sqm commercial.
- Engineering sources: three towers; A ~47F, B ~14F, C ~12F.
- Ping An Bank's 2026 financial license places the national Credit Card Center at A36–38, A40–44 and B5–14.
- The Shenzhen sub-center is separately licensed at A35.
- Ping An Fund publicly lists a subsidiary at A23.
- The C tower contains Hampton by Hilton Shenzhen Nanshan Qianhai.
- Public leasing materials market A-tower office space externally.

## Interpretation

```
Credit Card Center
= anchor HQ / major occupier
!= entire complex
```

The institutional footprint is still large and plausible for a national-scale credit-card platform covering issuance, lending, acquiring, transaction processing, merchant operations, risk, collections and supporting technology.

## Diligence boundary

Public evidence does not currently establish:
- total headcount
- seat utilization
- exact floor-by-floor team allocation
- full tenant roster
- real occupancy rate

These should remain UNVERIFIED rather than inferred from the building's name.
